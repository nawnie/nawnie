## The work

I build local-first AI tools and operational systems through two connected projects:

- **AI Without Fear** makes AI understandable through lessons, field guides, local tools, and honest experiments.
- **AI Embedded Systems** applies that thinking to private AI, embedded systems, robotics, and automation for real operations.

The repos are separate because the work is different. The throughline is not: "look what AI can do." It is: "here is the system, here is the evidence, and here is where a human still decides."

## What I care about

- private and local AI;
- consumer GPU workflows that can survive real Windows machines;
- model routing, retrieval, provenance, and evaluation;
- embedded and robotics systems;
- automation that records what it did and asks before it crosses a boundary.

The impressive demo is useful. The useful system is better.

## Stack
